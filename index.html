Debugging & Learning

During the process, I encountered an unexpected output waveform. Instead of accepting the result, I investigated the circuit, checked the component models, verified the switching operation, and analyzed the MOSFET gate drive. Debugging the simulation helped me understand the importance of correct device models, switching topology, and transient analysis in power electronics.

Key Learnings

  • Transient response analysis in LTspice
  • Troubleshooting simulation models and circuit behavior

HARDWARE & PCB DC-DC Step-Up PCB KiCad EDA v8

Boost Converter PCB Design in KiCad

Custom DC-DC step-up switching regulator PCB designed, routed, and 3D modeled in KiCad.

Controller MC34063AP
Inductor 100 µH
Diode 1N5822 Schottky
Filtering 470 µF Low-ESR

Designed and laid out a custom DC-DC Boost Converter PCB based on the MC34063AP switching controller. Built complete schematic capture, low-inductance switching loops, and 3D raytraced visualization.

Hardware & PCB Specifications

Input Voltage Range 5 V – 12 V DC
Output Voltage Range Adjustable (~10 V – 28 V)
Switching Controller IC MC34063AP (DIP-8)
Power Inductor 100 µH Drum Core
Schottky Rectifier Diode 1N5822 (3A, 40V, Low Vf)
Timing Capacitor (Ct) 1.5 nF (Pin 3 TC)
Current Sense (Rsc) 0.22 Ω (Pin 7 Ipk Limit)
Output Filter (C2) 470 µF Low-ESR
Input Filter (C1) 100 µF Electrolytic
Adjustment Trimmer 20 kΩ Potentiometer + 1 kΩ
Screw Terminals 2-Pin 5.08mm (Vin & Vout)
PCB Layer Count 2 Layers (Top/Bottom Cu)

PCB Layout & Routing Insights

Optimized the high-current switching loop between inductor L1, switch pin, catch diode D1, and reservoir capacitor C2 to minimize parasitic trace inductance and EMI. Kept the high-impedance feedback pin 5 isolated from switching nodes, with custom silkscreen branding (Mugilan 19-10-2026 First PCB) and full 3D clearance checks.

Key Engineering Learnings

  • Complete KiCad EDA workflow: Schematic capture, symbol annotation, and netlist generation
  • Two-layer PCB track routing with power net sizing and low-impedance ground returns
  • MC34063AP oscillator frequency tuning and feedback divider calculations
  • Component footprint pitch verification and 3D raytraced clearance checks
  • Silkscreen polarity labeling and terminal block safety layout
